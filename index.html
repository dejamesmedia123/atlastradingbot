FAQ

Common questions

Which countries can pay with Flutterwave?

Nigeria and a set of other supported African countries, configured by the admin team. Everywhere else, deposits go through manual bank transfer or crypto with proof, approved by an admin.

Is my deposit locked forever?

No — each bot sets its own withdrawal lock period, shown before you deposit. Once it passes, you can withdraw down to that bot's minimum withdrawal amount, confirmed with a one-time code sent via Telegram.

How does the affiliate commission actually get paid?

Commission is credited only when a referred deposit is confirmed, never on signup alone. Once your pending balance passes $20, request a payout and an admin settles it — automatically via Flutterwave where your bank details are on file, or manually otherwise.

Who decides which bots are available?

The AtlasTradeAI admin team adds, edits, and retires bots — setting deposit limits, profit share, and risk level for each one before it goes live.
